hello!

📢 I'm currently a Writing-up Fellow at the Konrad Lorenz Institute for Evolution and Cognition Research in Vienna ☕, where I'm preparing my doctoral thesis for submission.

I'm a philosopher and cognitive scientist working on the topic of agency. My PhD — based jointly at the Faculty of Sciences, University of Lisbon and the Institute for Philosophy II at Ruhr-Universität Bochum — takes a theoretical and experimental approach to the comparative problem of agency. I'm especially interested in the comparative scope of reinforcement learning as a framework for operationalising agency, and in how constraints and divergent evolutionary histories shape the control processes that enable agential behaviour across different species.

Before Vienna, I read for an MSc in Cognitive Neuroscience at Sussex, an MA in Philosophy of Mind & Cognitive Science at Birmingham, and a BA in Philosophy at Heythrop College, University of London. I've also worked as an affect researcher at Emteq Labs and as a research and editorial assistant to Erik Hoel.

Alongside my research, I care a great deal about building interdisciplinary research communities, science communication, and science reform. I'm a co-founder of the Non-Neural Cognition Lab, an interdisciplinary group studying agency and cognition beyond neural systems (with a particular fondness for Physarum polycephalum), and I serve as Vice President of the International Society for the Philosophy of the Sciences of the Mind. I'm also a member of the PPI Group for Interoception and Mental Health at the University of Surrey.

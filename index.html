hello!

📢 I recently completed a Writing-up Fellowship at the Konrad Lorenz Institute for Evolution and Cognition Research in Vienna, where I was preparing my doctoral thesis for submission.

I'm a philosopher and cognitive scientist working on the topic of agency. I am preparing to defend my PhD, titled "Reinforcement learning and the organisation of agency: a constraint-theoretic account", which has been based at the Faculty of Sciences, University of Lisbon and the Institute for Philosophy II at Ruhr-Universität Bochum. In it, I take a theoretical and experimental approach to the comparative problem of agency. I'm particularly interested in the explanatory scope of reinforcement learning as a framework for operationalising agency, and in how constraints and divergent evolutionary histories shape the control processes that enable agential behaviour across different species.

Before my PhD, I read for an MSc in Cognitive Neuroscience at Sussex, an MA in Philosophy of Mind & Cognitive Science at Birmingham, and a BA in Philosophy at Heythrop College, University of London. I also worked at as a researcher at a tech-startup and as a research and editorial assistant on a popular science book.

Alongside my research, I care a great deal about building interdisciplinary research communities, science communication, open science and science reform, as well efforts to bring the arts and humanities into kinship with the sciences. I'm a co-founder of the Non-Neural Cognition Lab, an interdisciplinary research group studying agency and cognition beyond the conventional neural systems we are typically biased toward (I harbour a particular fondness for Physarum polycephalum), and I serve as Vice President of the International Society for the Philosophy of the Sciences of the Mind. I'm also a member of the PPI Group for Interoception and Mental Health at the University of Surrey.
